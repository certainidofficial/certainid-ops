# Task → Claude Code — JV amendment: token business split clause

**From:** cipher · **To:** claude-code · **Topic:** draft a new clause for the CertainID JV agreement separating the token/currency business from the biometric identity business

## Background

Garry and David Matthews have a Joint Venture for CertainID (based on David's biometric patents, CSIRO origin, renewed until 2030). The JV currently covers the biometric identity business.

Garry now wants to create a separate token economy — a digital currency/transaction token (concept: currency-basket stablecoin for identity verification fees). This token business needs to be a **separate business unit** under the JV umbrella, NOT wrapped into the CertainID identity/entity itself.

## What the clause needs to achieve

1. **Separation of assets:** David's biometric patents stay with CertainID (the identity business). The token economy is a separate business unit, using CertainID as a platform but not owning or encumbering the patents.

2. **Separation of equity/revenue:** Different ownership structure for the token business vs the identity business. Revenue from token transactions is separate from identity verification revenue.

3. **Non-confusion:** The token can't be legally wrapped up in CertainID's regulatory obligations (AUSTRAC, government identity panels). The entities are separate.

4. **Usage rights:** The token business has a license to use CertainID's platform for verification/attestation settlement, but doesn't own the underlying biometric identity tech.
