## The Narrative

### The 30-second pitch

> *"myID and ConnectID are last decade's solution. They replaced paper documents with digital documents — your identity still lives on someone else's server. CertainID is the only system where your biometrics never leave your phone. There is no database to breach. No bank to approve you. The verification hash lives on the blockchain — permanent, public, provable by anyone. Your face should verify you. Not a bank. Not a government server."*

### The Three Wedges

**Wedge 1 — Privacy (vs myID)**
- myID: upload your passport + licence to the government. Now the government knows every time you verify.
- CertainID: biometrics never leave your phone. Zero-knowledge. The government doesn't know when, where, or how often you verify.

**Wedge 2 — Independence (vs ConnectID)**
- ConnectID: only works if you have a participating bank. What if you're unbanked? What if you switch banks? What if the exchange gets breached?
- CertainID: your identity is on your device. No bank needed. No exchange needed. Just you and your phone.

**Wedge 3 — Permanence (vs both)**
- Both systems: identity lives in a database you don't control. Government can revoke. Bank can close your account. Verifiable only within their system.
- CertainID: attestation hash on-chain. Permanent. Immutable. Verifiable by anyone, anywhere, forever. You own it.

## The ConnectID Challenge

ConnectID is well-funded (4 big banks), expanding fast (Snapchat, Telstra, rental apps, vehicle sales, NDIS), and has government accreditation.

**Our counter:**
- They're centralized. We're not. That's the whole argument.
- The Snapchat age check doesn't need a bank — it needs proof of age. CertainID could do that with just a device.
- The rental application pilot doesn't need bank data — it needs verified identity. CertainID could do that with no document upload at all.
- **ConnectID is "identity for the banked." CertainID is "identity for everyone."**
