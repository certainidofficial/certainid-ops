## 2. The three ways we could work with Meta

### Option A — Platform verification layer (biggest, slowest)
- CertainID ownership deeds plug into Meta's verification/trust processes
- A real account carries an on-chain deed + biometric-verified human → Meta's bots check the deed before banning
- Fake account report: the claimant must hold the title. No deed, no claim, no takedown of the real one.
- Partnership, not purchase: Meta reduces false bans + fakes; we get adoption at scale.

### Option B — Creator safety product (fastest, no permission needed)
- Position CertainID as protection FOR creators on Meta's platforms
- "Get your ownership deed. If Meta's bots ever take you down, present your deed on-chain and get restored."
- Meta doesn't need to be technically involved — it's a user-side tool that reduces their support burden
- **We can ship this without Meta's blessing.** The planning question is when to pitch them after traction.

### Option C — Legal/compliance evidence layer
- New SA/QLD/NSW deepfake laws are landing; offenders are overseas; platforms get subpoenaed constantly
- A deed provides independent, court-ready proof of "who owns what" — reduces Meta's legal/compliance load
- Angle: "we are the infrastructure that makes compliance with new deepfake laws cheaper and provable."
